# Vision — Donald Mayes Ministries

Read this before changing major UX, content, or positioning.

## Who this is

The long-term digital home of **Pastor Donald Mayes** and **Donald Mayes Ministries**.

Pastor Mayes is the human center of the experience. The site is not a church website, megachurch template, nonprofit template, or marketing funnel.

## Central message

**Loving Everyone Always.**

This phrase is a brand asset. Keep it prominent and intact.

## Mission

Donald Mayes Ministries exists to teach followers of Christ how to love people through practical biblical living.

Short form: **Love people through practical biblical living.**

## Vision

To cross denominational, racial, and theological lines with God's love through the power of reconciliation and restoration.

Mission = what the ministry does. Vision = what that work hopes to accomplish.

## Core values — F.C.L.

- **Faithful to God.**
- **Committed to Serving.**
- **Loyal to the Body of Christ.**

Supporting identity: **Faithful. Committed. Loyal.**

## Audience

**People who care about people.**

Donald Mayes Ministries is for people who want their faith to shape the way they treat others.

## Pastor-centric strategy

- Pastor Mayes should be immediately recognizable as the face of the ministry
- Do not center church buildings or generic ministry stock
- Do not hide him behind abstract branding
- Photography placeholders exist until real portraits arrive; do not invent a likeness

## Site tone

Personal, mature, credible, pastoral, contemporary, exceptionally clean.

Editorial and human. Not SaaS, not flashy, not aggressive.

## Phase 1 objectives

Three primary pages:

1. **Home / About** — Who is Donald Mayes, what the ministry is, how to connect
2. **Speaking** — professional speaker one-sheet + booking form
3. **Prayer Requests** — personal, warm, simple prayer form

Plus Privacy, Terms, and 404.

Navigation stays: **Home | Speaking | Prayer Requests | Book Pastor Mayes**

## Page purposes

- Home answers identity, message, and connection quickly
- Speaking is conversion-aware without feeling sales-heavy
- Prayer must feel different: calm, safe, human. Not a lead form

## Design principles

- Typography, whitespace, hierarchy over cards and decoration
- Locked brand tokens in `src/app/globals.css`; do not scatter hex values
- Subtle motion only; honor `prefers-reduced-motion`
- Accessibility is required, not optional

## What this site must not become

- A church organization site
- A program catalog
- A testimonial mill with invented social proof
- A CRM/automation product
- A generic Christian stock-photo brand
- An overdesigned portfolio or AI-looking template

Protect the client's words. Do not rewrite ministry language into marketing copy.
